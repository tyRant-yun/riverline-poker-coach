Formula definitions: `call_cost` is the legal call's `cost` amount or zero when
no call exists; `pot_odds = call_cost / (current_pot + call_cost)` when cost is
positive, otherwise `0` with `no_call_cost`.  `SPR = effective_stack / current
pre-action pot`, where effective stack is the minimum remaining stack among
active seats; it is `null` with `undefined_zero_pot` for a zero pot.  Bet is
reported as `by`, raise as `to`, and their inclusive legal endpoints are never
converted between those meanings.
